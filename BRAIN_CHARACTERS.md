# MillionDollar Brain Characters and Moods

**Catalog:** v3  
**Characters:** 42  
**Closed moods per character:** 5

## Design contract

Each NFT has two simultaneous identities:

1. A memorable character for people: name, glyph, crew, tagline, lore, and mood names.
2. A precise control for the allocator: immutable affinity, influence weight, signed mood value, data dependencies, tags, and canonical instruction.

Flavor text never enters the executable asset allowlist and cannot override risk limits. Owners cannot write prompts. The selected mood ID is on-chain; versioned application code resolves it to its exact signed effect and instruction.

The five mood positions always run from the affinity's first pole at `-2`, through neutral at `0`, to its second pole at `+2`.

## Memeability without prompt injection

- Character names and lore are protocol-authored and versioned.
- A mood change emits the same on-chain preset event as any other setting change.
- Social language never becomes an asset address, swap command, tool call, or untrusted instruction.
- The allocator receives the character name and mood for explainability, plus the canonical instruction and signed intensity for decision-making.
- Missing structured data still deactivates only the dependent effect, even if its character and lore remain visible.
